# Evidence brief
As of 27 October 2026. These are verified public source extracts summarized in our own words, not company evidence.
S1 Rogers, Everett M. (2003), Diffusion of Innovations, fifth edition, Free Press. Publisher excerpt: https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Diffusion-of-Innovations-5th-Edition/Everett-M-Rogers/9780743222099 and fourth edition author excerpt confirming the five attributes: https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Diffusion-of-Innovations-4th-Edition/Everett-M-Rogers/9781451602470 . Framework, not empirical evidence for generative AI.
S2 Bick, Blandin and Deming, RPS via FRED, series RPSGENAIUSAGESHAREWORK: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/RPSGENAIUSAGESHAREWORK . Updated 20 October 2026. Q3 2026 40.54550%; Q2 45.19932%; Q3 2025 37.35947%. US employed adults aged 18–64 reporting generative AI use for work; quarterly, not seasonally adjusted, self-reported. Does not measure firm deployment, customer-support use or use intensity. Latest quarter declined; long-run increase does not establish uninterrupted growth. No significance claim without sampling uncertainty.
S3 Grundy, Breaux and Khatiwoda, US Census Bureau, Large Firms With at Least 20 Employees Biggest AI Users, 26 May 2026: https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/ai-use-businesses.html . For period ending 3 May 2026: 32% of businesses with 100–249 employees used AI; Information sector 39.7%; national 19.8%. These are separate marginal groups, not the joint software-by-size peer group. Definition is AI in any business function in past two weeks, not specifically generative AI support. November 2025 question change limits historical comparability. Newer BTOS downloads existed by 22 October 2026 (https://www.census.gov/hfp/btos/data_downloads); their workbooks were not analyzed here. May values are dated context, not latest penetration.
S4 Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, Generative AI at Work, NBER working paper 31161 (2023), November 2023 version summarized by Stanford GSB: https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/working-papers/generative-ai-work and https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161 . Author-hosted indexed abstract reports 5,179 customer-support agents and 14% average improvement in issues resolved per hour for an assistant. Supports relative advantage in one deployment, not current prevalence, autonomous reliability or a forecast for this organization. Direct page fetches returned 403; verification was limited to indexed original-source abstracts. Version differences are possible; do not mix later versions or subgroup estimates.
S5 Autio et al., NIST AI 600-1, Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile, 26 July 2024: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence ; https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1 . Voluntary companion to AI RMF, not certification, legal clearance or deployment prevalence. Used as governance guidance only.
