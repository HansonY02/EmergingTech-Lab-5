# Toy Example - Environmental Sustainability Screening

This example is NOT one of the seven graded specialist agents. It demonstrates how a specialist narrows the common scaffold.

Assess the technology's material energy/resource footprint and credible lifecycle concerns generally; then interpret those concerns for the intended application; then interpret them for the organization's stated sustainability commitments, operating scale, constraints, and decision horizon. Distinguish measured evidence from estimates. Do not claim a complete lifecycle assessment unless the evidence supports one. Ask for more information when scale, energy source, utilization, or replacement cycle materially affects the conclusion.
